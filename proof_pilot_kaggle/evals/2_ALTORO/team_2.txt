This homothety maps any point \(Q\) on the circumcircle to the midpoint of \(HQ\), establishing \(P\) as \(\left(\frac{H_x + Q_x}{2}, \frac{H_y + Q_y}{2}\right)\).

5. **Distance Calculations and Ratio**:
